Grade 6 English Grammar Guide

Everything you need to know about the Present Simple and Present Continuous tenses, their differences, sentence structures, and signal words.

Tense 1

The Present Simple Tense

🎯 When to Use Present Simple:

  • Habits and daily routines: Things you do repeatedly (e.g., getting up, doing homework, brushing teeth).
  • Regular occurrences: Things that happen regularly on specific days or times.
  • Facts and general truths: Scientific laws, nature, and things that are always true.

🏗️ Sentence Structures:

Positive (+)
Subject + base verb (add -s / -es for he, she, it)
• I play football every day.
• She goes to school by bus.
Negative (−)
Subject + do / does not + base verb
• They do not watch TV in the morning.
• He does not eat meat.
Question (?)
Do / Does + subject + base verb?
• Do you like pizza?
• Does he like English?

✨ Examples:

⚽ I play football every day. (Daily routine)
🚌 She goes to school by bus. (Regular habit; notice go + -es for 'she')
📺 They do not watch TV in the morning. (Negative form with 'do not')
❓ Does he like English? (Question form with 'Does'; verb 'like' stays in base form)

🏷️ Guide Words / Signal Words for Present Simple:

When you see these signal words, they almost always point to the Present Simple!

always usually often sometimes rarely never every day every week on Mondays
Tense 2

The Present Continuous Tense

🎯 When to Use Present Continuous:

  • Actions happening right now: Activities occurring at the exact moment of speaking.
  • Temporary actions: Things happening around the present time (e.g., this week, today) that are not permanent.

🏗️ Sentence Structures:

Positive (+)
Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing
• I am doing my homework now.
• She is reading a book at the moment.
• They are playing football right now.
Negative (−)
Subject + am / is / are + not + verb-ing
• I am not sleeping.
• He is not (isn't) listening to music.
• We are not (aren't) watching TV.
Question (?)
Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?
• Are you listening to the teacher?
• Is he reading a comic?

✨ Examples:

📝 I am doing my homework now. ('I' takes 'am' + doing)
📖 She is reading a book at the moment. ('She' takes 'is' + reading)
⚽ They are playing football right now. ('They' takes 'are' + playing)
👂 Are you listening to the teacher? (Question form with 'Are' + you + listening)

🏷️ Guide Words / Signal Words for Present Continuous:

Look out for these clue words telling you something is happening at or around now!

now right now at the moment today currently Look! Listen!

Grade 6 Secrets

Spelling Rules & Stative Verbs

🔤 Spelling Rules for Present Simple (-s / -es)

Most verbs: Just add -s.
play → plays, read → reads, eat → eats
Verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o: Add -es.
watch → watches, go → goes, teach → teaches
Consonant + y: Change y to -ies.
study → studies, fly → flies

🔤 Spelling Rules for -ing

Verbs ending in silent -e: Drop the -e and add -ing.
ride → riding, make → making, write → writing
Short verb (Consonant + Vowel + Consonant): Double the final letter.
swim → swimming, run → running, sit → sitting

🧠 Stative Verbs (NO -ing!)

Some verbs describe feelings, thoughts, or states rather than physical actions. These are called Stative Verbs. We use them in the Present Simple even if we mean right now!

understand ✅ I understand now. ❌ I am understanding now.
know ✅ She knows the answer. ❌ She is knowing.
like / love ✅ They like music. ❌ They are liking music.
want ✅ He wants some water. ❌ He is wanting.
